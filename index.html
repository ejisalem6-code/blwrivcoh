Who we are

More than a campus fellowship.

BLW RIVCOH is the Believers' LoveWorld campus fellowship at Rivers State College of Health — a chapter of the wider LoveWorld family, built around powerful worship, impactful teaching, and a loving community.

Beyond Sunday and Wednesday services, we run friendship circles and prayer meetings that help members connect personally and stay accountable in their walk with God.
