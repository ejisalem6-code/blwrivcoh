About Us

We are BLW RIVCOH.

The Believers' LoveWorld Fellowship at Rivers State College of Health — a chapter of the wider LoveWorld family, built around powerful worship, impactful teaching, and a loving community.

Our story

Welcome to our fellowship family.

A place where believers grow in God's Word, build meaningful relationships, and experience the transforming power of the Holy Spirit.

Beyond Sunday and Wednesday services, we run friendship circles and prayer meetings that help members connect personally and stay accountable in their walk with God — because campus life is better lived together.
